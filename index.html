stockracers
Hold it. Watch it. Get paid in stock.
Every 15 minutes, holders are split into four teams and race with real Robinhood stocks. The winning team gets that run's trading fees, bought as the team's best stock and sent straight to their wallets.
1 · The deal20 Robinhood stocks are dealt into 4 groups and every holder into 4 teams. You watch the deal happen before each run.
2 · The runFor 15 minutes, each team's score is the sum of its 5 stocks' moves. Highest score at the close wins.
3 · The potTrading fees from the run buy the winning group's best-performing stock, as a real Robinhood stock token.
4 · The payoutThe stock is sent to the winning team's wallets by how much each one holds. Nothing to claim, nothing to connect.
How payouts work
Payouts are batched on Robinhood Chain. Your share accrues as credit and is sent once it reaches $2, so gas never eats a small win. The pot pays the gas, not you.
Every payout is a public transaction. Click any past run to see the batches, the wallets and the split.
Teams and stock groups are re-dealt every run. Losing one run costs nothing; the next deal starts in a minute.
Only holding counts. Selling before the close takes you out of that run's team.
